## Hear it. Read it. Gone.

Said creates private, on-device English captions for audio playing through your
Mac. This alpha includes the native menu-bar app, audio-only system capture,
local Parakeet streaming, stable two-line captions, and the polished caption
controls.

### Install

1. Download **Said-0.1.0-alpha.dmg** below.
2. Open it and drag **Said** into **Applications**.
3. On first launch, Control-click Said and choose **Open**, then confirm.
4. Allow **System Audio Recording Only** and let Said download and verify its
   local speech model once.

### Requirements

- Apple silicon Mac.
- macOS 26 or later.
- English system audio.

### Alpha signing notice
